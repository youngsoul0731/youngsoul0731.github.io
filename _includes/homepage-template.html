About me.

Connecting digital agents with the physical world.

I am a PhD student at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, supervised by Prof. Siheng Chen. My research interests lie in LLM agent applications, with a current focus on integrating LLM agents into embodied agent development.

I am interested in how agents use tools, follow instructions, and support the development of embodied intelligence. If you are interested in collaborating, feel free to contact me at youngsoul0731@gmail.com.
