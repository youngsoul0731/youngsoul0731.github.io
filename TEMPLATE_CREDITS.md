# Template source

The homepage layout and `styles.css` are adapted from **Jingbo Wang’s homepage**:

- Source: https://github.com/wangjingbo1219/wangjingbo1219.github.io
- Reference website: https://wangjingbo1219.github.io/
- Retrieved: September 14, 2026

The adaptation retains the reference’s monochrome palette, numbered navigation,
portrait layout, section hierarchy, and research card styling. The homepage footer
links directly to the original repository.

Personal information, portrait, paper figures, and publication details come from
Yuanshuo Zhang’s existing homepage. Research is organized into Digital Agents,
Physical Agents, and Others; EmboCoach-Bench is listed in both agent groups.

The existing repository LICENSE is retained. This attribution does not assert a
new license for the reference template or third-party research figures.
